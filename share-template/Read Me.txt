ICONIC - a frosted-glass home for your desktop icons (Windows 11)

INSTALL
1. Right-click Iconic.zip and choose "Extract All".
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Install Iconic".
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   (It shows this for any app that isn't from a big publisher.)

Iconic opens and offers to tidy your desktop. Say yes and everything moves into the
Iconic window, leaving one Iconic icon on the desktop. Nothing is deleted: items are kept
in Documents\Iconic Library, and the ••• menu has "Put everything back on desktop".

TIPS
- Click an icon to open it. Type anywhere to search; Enter opens the first match.
- Drag icons to rearrange them or move them between groups.
- Right-click an icon to rename it, change its picture or move it to another group.
- Right-click a group's title to rename it, recolor, sort, reorder or delete it.
- The pin button keeps the window open when you click away (handy for dragging files in).
- ••• menu: add apps, tidy the desktop, Start with Windows, quit.
